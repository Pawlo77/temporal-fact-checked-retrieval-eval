# Qwen3 Embedding

> [!info] TL;DR
> A technical report releasing embedding and pointwise reranking models at 0.6B, 4B and 8B parameters, all initialised from Qwen3 language models with 32K context under Apache 2.0. The embedders are pre-trained on about 150M synthetic pairs generated by Qwen3-32B, fine-tuned on about 7M labelled and 12M filtered synthetic pairs, then merged. Qwen3-Embedding-8B scores 70.58 on MTEB Multilingual against 63.22 for multilingual-e5-large-instruct. Qwen3-Reranker-8B reaches 72.94 on the multilingual retrieval subset vs 58.36 for bge-reranker-v2-m3.

## Motivation

- Encoder-only embedders such as [[Multilingual E5]] and [[BGE-M3]] gather weak supervision from web pairs (forums, papers), which is hard to control for task, language or difficulty.
- LLM backbones bring more world knowledge and instruction following, and had begun to be used for embedding and reranking (the predecessor GTE-Qwen series, [Gemini Embedding, Lee et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07891)).
- Low-resource languages lack natural training pairs, which synthetic generation can supply.

## Method

Notation: post $q$, fact-check $f$ (the report's document $d$), task instruction $I$, temperature $\tau$.

**Embedder.** A causal LLM reads "$I$ $q$" followed by an end token; the last-layer hidden state of that token is the embedding. Fact-checks are encoded without instruction. Dimensions are 1024, 2560 and 4096 by size, with custom lower dimensions supported.

**Embedding loss.** InfoNCE with cosine similarity $s$. The denominator includes the positive, $K$ hard negatives, in-batch queries and in-batch documents. Any in-batch item scoring more than 0.1 above the positive is masked out as a likely false negative.

Intuition: many in-batch comparisons sharpen the space, and the mask avoids punishing the model for duplicates of the right answer.

**Reranker.** The LLM gets a chat prompt with instruction, query and document and must answer "yes" or "no". The score is $\frac{e^{P(\text{yes})}}{e^{P(\text{yes})} + e^{P(\text{no})}}$, trained with a supervised fine-tuning loss on the label.

**Training pipeline.** Embedders: weakly supervised pre-training on synthetic pairs, supervised fine-tuning, then slerp merging of fine-tuning checkpoints. Rerankers skip the first stage.

**Synthetic data.** Qwen3-32B writes queries for passages from the Qwen3 pre-training corpus. It first picks a persona (top-5 candidates retrieved from Persona Hub), question type and difficulty, then length and language. Pairs with cosine similarity above 0.7 are kept for fine-tuning.

## Key numbers

- MTEB Multilingual (131 tasks, mean task score): Qwen3-Embedding-8B 70.58, 4B 69.45, 0.6B 64.33. Baselines from the leaderboard on 4 June 2025 are Gemini Embedding 68.37, multilingual-e5-large-instruct 63.22 and BGE-M3 59.56.
- MTEB English v2: 8B 75.22 vs multilingual-e5-large-instruct 65.53; MTEB Code: 8B 80.68.
- Reranking the top 100 of Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B on the MMTEB retrieval subset: embedder alone 64.64, bge-reranker-v2-m3 58.36, Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B 66.36, 4B 72.74, 8B 72.94.

## Experiments

- **Embedding benchmarks:** 216 tasks across MTEB Multilingual, MTEB English v2, CMTEB and MTEB Code; 4B and 8B lead every suite, 0.6B trails only Gemini Embedding on the multilingual suite.
- **Reranking:** six retrieval suites (English, Chinese, multilingual, MLDR, code, FollowIR). All Qwen3 rerankers beat the embedder and the baselines on the multilingual suite. bge-reranker-v2-m3 is below the embedder on English, multilingual and code retrieval. The 4B reranker is best on English retrieval (69.76) and FollowIR (14.84).
- **Ablation (0.6B embedder, MMTEB):** synthetic pre-training only 58.49; no synthetic data 61.21; no merging 62.56; full pipeline 64.33.

## Data

- Weak supervision: about 150M synthetic pairs for retrieval, bitext mining, classification and semantic similarity.
- Fine-tuning: about 7M labelled pairs (MS MARCO, NQ, HotpotQA, NLI, DuReader, T2-Ranking, SimCLUE, MIRACL, MLDR, Mr. TyDi, Multi-CPR, CodeSearchNet and others) plus about 12M synthetic pairs.

> [!failure] Limitations
> - Technical report without peer review; baseline scores are copied from a leaderboard, not rerun.
> - No per-language results, so performance on Polish is not reported.
> - No statement of the training-data cutoff of the Qwen3 backbone or of the synthetic corpus.
> - Reranker evaluation uses one first-stage retriever (Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B), and gains from 4B to 8B are small or negative on some suites.
> - Queries are synthesised from the same pre-training corpus that trains the backbone, so evaluation sets in that corpus could be memorised; the report does not discuss contamination.

## Relation to our project

- Qwen3-Embedding-8B and Qwen3-Reranker-8B are our LLM-scale systems ([[Proposed Task]], [[Enhanced topic research]]).
- Released in June 2025, after MultiClaim and the SemEval-2025 test period, with no stated cutoff. The backbone may have read fact-checks published after many test posts, so these models anchor the parametric-leakage strata of RQ5 ([[Retrieval Models]]).
- Its reranker table shows bge-reranker-v2-m3 can fall below a strong embedder, so we report the embedder alone next to every reranked run.
- No Polish breakdown means Polish quality must come from our own runs and from [[PIRB]]-style evidence ([[Language scope analysis]]).

## Future work

- **Partly done:** independent per-language evaluation; [[polish-reranker-roberta-v3]] compares Qwen3-Reranker-8B on 41 Polish tasks and reports the Polish model ahead on 18 of them.
- **Open:** a stated training-data cutoff, or a probe of what the backbone knows about dated events, for the Qwen3 embedder and reranker (searched 2026-10).
- **Open:** contamination analysis of the synthetic pre-training pairs against public retrieval benchmarks (searched 2026-10).
